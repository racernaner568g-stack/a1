Haute Maroquinerie Craft

The Heritage & Science of Luxury Handbags

Stationed in Soho, Manhattan, PoshCarrySpot was established to champion hand saddle-stitching, evaluate full-grain vegetable-tanned leathers, and provide an empirical guide for handbag connoisseurs.

Atelier Mandate

Authentic Hand Craftsmanship Over Fast-Fashion Synthetic Substitutes

In a fashion industry inundated with cheap polyurethane pleather and disposable plastic accessories, PoshCarrySpot advocates for hand-waxed linen saddle-stitching, 24K gold-plated solid brass, and 100% full-grain calfskin.

Our team of master leather artisans, auction house handbag specialists, and tannery directors evaluate carryalls through rigorous laboratory trials: testing seam tensile strength, edge-paint flexibility, and patina development over time.

"A true luxury handbag is not merely a carryall—it is an heirloom of master craftsmanship, structural balance, and enduring patina."

Craft Principles

Our Four Haute Maroquinerie Standards

1. Hand Saddle Stitch

Double-needle hand stitching with waxed linen thread for maximum seam durability.

2. Vegetable Tanning

Natural plant extracts developing warm, rich patina across decades of carry.

3. Jewelry Hardware

Heavyweight solid brass latches electroplated in 24K gold or palladium finish.

4. Objective Research

100% independent handbag evaluations backed by auction data and leather stress tests.
